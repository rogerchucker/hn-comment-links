# Privacy Policy

**HN Comment Links** values your privacy.  

- **No data collection**: This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or browsing information.  
- **Local processing only**: All functionality—extracting hyperlinks from Hacker News comment sections and displaying them in the popup—happens entirely within your browser.  
- **Permissions use**:  
  - `activeTab` and `host permission` are used only to temporarily access the current Hacker News discussion page when you click the extension.  
  - `tabs` is used solely to check the current tab’s URL to enable/disable the extension and update the badge count.  
  - `clipboardWrite` is used only when you explicitly copy extracted links.  

The extension does not communicate with any external servers, and no information leaves your device.  

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the project’s GitHub repository.
